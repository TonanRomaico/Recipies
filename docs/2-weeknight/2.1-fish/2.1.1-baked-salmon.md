# 2.1.1 One-dish baked salmon

Salmon with burst cherry tomatoes and asparagus, all baked in one skillet or glass dish, with toasted baguette to mop up the juices.

| Serves | Active time | Total time | Equipment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 10 min | ~30 min | Oven-safe skillet or glass baking dish |

!!! info "Teaches"
    [Timing ingredients in the oven](../../techniques/oven-timing.md): the vegetables go in first, and the salmon joins them partway through.

## Shopping list

- [ ] 2 salmon fillets (~170 g / 6 oz each)
- [ ] 300 g (10 oz) cherry tomatoes
- [ ] 1 bunch (~450 g / 1 lb) asparagus *(or zucchini / green beans)*
- [ ] 1 head of garlic
- [ ] 1 lemon
- [ ] Butter
- [ ] Baguette
- [ ] *Pantry:* olive oil, salt, black pepper

## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets
- 300 g cherry tomatoes
- 450 g asparagus, woody ends snapped off
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 lemon: half sliced, half for juice
- 2 tbsp butter
- ½ baguette, sliced

## Steps

1. **Preheat.** Heat the oven to 200 °C / 400 °F. Take the salmon out of the fridge while it heats. *(~10 min)*
2. **Give the vegetables a head start.** In the skillet or dish, toss the tomatoes, asparagus and garlic with 2 tbsp oil and half the salt and pepper. Roast until the tomatoes start to soften. *(10 min)*
3. **Prep the salmon.** While the vegetables roast, pat the salmon dry, season it with the rest of the salt and pepper, and rub it with the remaining oil.
4. **Bake the salmon.** Push the vegetables to the sides and nestle the salmon in the middle. Top each fillet with lemon slices and a pat of butter. Bake until it flakes easily with a fork. *(12 min)*
5. **Toast the bread.** For the last 5 minutes, put the baguette slices straight on the oven rack.
6. **Finish.** Squeeze the remaining lemon over everything. Rub a roasted garlic clove onto the bread, crush the burst tomatoes into the pan juices, and mop them up.

!!! tip "Doneness"
    Salmon is moist at 50–55 °C / 125–130 °F in the center, or fully firm at 63 °C / 145 °F.

!!! warning "Glass dishes"
    Only put a glass dish into an oven that's already hot. Never set a hot one on a wet or cold surface, and never use it under the broiler. Sudden temperature changes can crack it.
