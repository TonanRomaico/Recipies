# Timing ingredients in the oven

Different ingredients need different amounts of time. To make them all finish together in one dish, **start the slowest ones first** and add the faster ones later.

| Ingredient | Roughly at 200 °C / 400 °F |
|---|---|
| Potatoes, carrots (chunks) | 30–40 min |
| Cherry tomatoes, asparagus, zucchini | 15–20 min |
| Salmon fillet | 12–15 min |
| Bread slices (toasting) | 4–6 min |

Work backwards from the fastest item: the salmon needs 12 min and the vegetables about 22, so the vegetables go in 10 minutes earlier.
